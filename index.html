Investment Strategy

Vineyard Investment Group maintains a disciplined acquisition strategy focused on multifamily communities where operational improvements, thoughtful renovations, and strategic asset management create lasting value. Our clearly defined buying profile allows us to evaluate opportunities efficiently, respond quickly, and execute with confidence.

Core Buying Profile

  • Asset Type: Multifamily / Apartments
  • Unit Count: 20+ Units
  • Vintage: Ideally 1970 and Newer
  • Target Markets: Texas & Georgia
  • Primary Metros: Dallas/Fort Worth, Houston & Atlanta
  • Investment Horizon: 5–7+ Years

Value Creation Focus

  • Class B & C Multifamily Communities
  • Value-Add Investment Opportunities
  • Assets Below 90% Occupancy
  • Acquisitions Below Stabilized Market Value
  • 10%+ Target Returns
  • Clear Operational & Renovation Upside
